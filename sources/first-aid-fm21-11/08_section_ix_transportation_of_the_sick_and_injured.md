# Section IX: Transportation Of The Sick And Injured

3 65. GENERAL.-Correct transportation of a seriously in-
jured person is one of the most important parts of first aid.
Careless or rough handling can increase the seriousness of
an injury and may even cause death. Whenever possible,
give whatever first aid is needed before moving an injured
person. If there is no absolute need for moving him at once,
it is usually best to let him stay where he is until he can be
moved by litter, ambulance, or some other means. Unless
he is already overheated, an injured person should be kept
warm both while waiting to be moved and during transporta-
tion.
